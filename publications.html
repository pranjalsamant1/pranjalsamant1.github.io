Vision-based manipulation of transparent plastic bags in industrial setups.

Frontiers in Robotics and AI · 27 January 2025

Helping a robot handle
what is hard to see.

Transparent, flexible packaging is difficult for robots to detect and manipulate. This research combines vision, depth sensing and a Franka robot arm to automate bag handling and cutting for an eight-stack bulk loader.

My contribution

Co-author, credited with software, methodology, validation, investigation and conceptualisation. This was a collaborative research contribution.

What the team demonstrated

A laboratory-validated system integrating bag detection, vacuum gripping, pick-and-place operations and cutting. The paper documents the methods, experiments and limitations.
