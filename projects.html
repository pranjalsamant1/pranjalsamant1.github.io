01 / Autonomy

Autonomous inspection. Measurable robot health.

Drone-in-a-Box · Independent project · Simulation · 2026

A simulated drone flies solar-farm inspection missions while RideScan monitors its behaviour. The question: can monitoring tell a developing motor fault apart from a passing gust of wind?

What I worked on

  • Integrated ROS 2, PX4 SITL, Gazebo Harmonic and MAVSDK for autonomous mission execution.
  • Recorded flight telemetry at 50 Hz; automated a 15-run healthy baseline and CSV validation.
  • Compared motor degradation with temporary wind disturbances. RideScan monitoring distinguished persistent degradation from a transient disturbance.

RideScan Integration Hackathon finalist · Featured Builder Case Study

02 / Robotics

Speak a command. Move a robot arm.

AR and voice robot programming · MSc dissertation · Simulation · 2024

A hands-free interface for robot programming. An operator speaks into a RealWear Navigator 520 AR headset; the application sends the command to ROS, and a simulated robot arm performs the requested movement.

What I worked on

  • Built the Unity Android headset application and connected it to ROS over Wi-Fi using ROSBridge and ROS#.
  • Used MoveIt to plan arm movements and Gazebo to simulate their execution.
  • Validated 60 of 60 commands across five sequences; measured 112.1 ms mean response latency over 30 separate latency trials.

FocusSummit. A Windows app, shipped.

Published Microsoft Store app · Three Baltic Mountains

What I worked on
